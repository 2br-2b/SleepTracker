SleepTracker automatically detects your sleep by monitoring screen lock and unlock events within configurable bedtime and wake-up windows. When you wake up, it prompts you to review and confirm the detected session before saving it to Health Connect.

Features:
- Automatic sleep detection using screen on/off events and time-window heuristics
- Configurable bedtime and wake-up windows
- Tracks awakenings during the night (screen unlocks and re-locks)
- Manual logging mode — set a daily template and adjust each morning
- Review and edit detected sessions before saving
- Writes SleepSessionRecord with sleep stages to Health Connect
- 7-day sleep history on the home screen
- Customisable sleep stage labels and emoji
- Reminder notifications to prompt morning review
- No tracking, no analytics, no accounts required

Requires Health Connect (built into Android 14+; available separately on Android 9–13).
